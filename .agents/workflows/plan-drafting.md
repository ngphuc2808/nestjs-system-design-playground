# Plan Drafting (workflow)

Draft a feature's plan.md from its resolved brainstorm.md/clarify.md, then present a short summary and explicitly ask the developer to decide proceed_to_task or back_to_clarify — the Plan-stage loop-back checkpoint. Refuses to draft while clarify.md still has open questions.

See `.agents/skills/plan-drafting/SKILL.md` for the full instructions.
